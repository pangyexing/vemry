# Unsent letters

## To Grandma, 27 September

You asked what I actually do all day. This week I read one paper, slowly, a chapter at a time, and tried to say each chapter back in my own words until I got it right. It is not so different from the way you taught me my times tables: say it, check it, say it again tomorrow.
