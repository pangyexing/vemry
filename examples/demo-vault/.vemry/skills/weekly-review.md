# Weekly review

When I ask for a weekly review, work from the index, not from memory.

1. `query_blocks` for tasks that are overdue or due in the next seven days.
2. `query_blocks` for claims, and tell me which have no evidence attached.
3. Read this week's `journal/` entries and name anything I said I would do and did not.
4. Write the result as one section appended to the current week's journal note —
   propose the edit, do not assume it.

Keep it under a page. A review I do not read is worse than none.
