# Cards — Remembering longer

From [[Remembering longer]]. A grade in the Review view is written back onto each card's last line, so this note is the deck.

> [!card] An hour after learning, how much of the original work did Ebbinghaus have to do again?
> About half; after eight hours, two thirds (from [[Ebbinghaus 1885, Chapter 7]]).
> [due:: 2026-09-30] [interval:: 1] [ease:: 2.5] [reviews:: 1] #card

> [!card] Why review just before you would forget, rather than right after learning?
> A review that catches the memory as it fades makes it last longer than it did the time before, so the next gap can be longer (from [[遗忘曲线与间隔重复]]).
> [due:: 2026-09-30] #card

> [!card] What decides how far to push the next review?
> How hard this recall was: a struggle brings the next review sooner, an easy one pushes it out. The Review view's four grades are that decision.
> [due:: 2026-09-30] #card
