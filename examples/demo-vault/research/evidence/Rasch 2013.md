# Rasch 2013

A review rather than an experiment, which makes it the right place to start and the wrong place to stop.

> [!evidence] Reactivation during sleep is observable, not inferred — replay has been recorded directly.
> supports:[[Sleep and Memory#^consolidation]]
> This is the strongest single argument against the passive account: something is happening, not merely not-happening.

> [!evidence] Targeted memory reactivation shifts what gets consolidated.
> supports:[[Sleep and Memory#^consolidation]]
> Cue a smell or a sound during slow-wave sleep and the associated material is recalled better. Consolidation is steerable, which is hard to explain passively.

Related: [[Walker 2017]], and the nap question in [[Sleep and Memory]].
