# Wiegreffe & Pinter 2019

*Attention is not not Explanation*, EMNLP 2019. A reply to [[Jain & Wallace 2019]].

> [!evidence] Weights swapped in by hand after training are not weights the model learned; a fair test trains a model to produce the alternative weights
> supports:[[Is attention explanation#^depends]]

> [!evidence] They first ask whether attention matters at all: where a model with uniform weights does about as well, there is little for the weights to explain
> supports:[[Is attention explanation#^depends]]

Their point, as I read it: "explanation" can mean plausible to a person or faithful to the computation, and a test for one does not settle the other.
