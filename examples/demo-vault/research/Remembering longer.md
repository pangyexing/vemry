# Remembering longer

It's not how often you review but when: once, just as it starts to fade, is the best deal.

## Excerpts

> [!quote] [Ebbinghaus 1885, Chapter 7](https://psychclassics.yorku.ca/Ebbinghaus/memory7.htm) · 2026-09-29
> One hour after the end of the learning, the forgetting had already progressed so far that one half the amount of the original work had to be expended before the series could be reproduced again; after 8 hours the work to be made up amounted to two thirds of the first effort.

## What the handout adds

[[遗忘曲线与间隔重复]], read in translation: review just as you are about to forget, and stretch the gap each time; how far to stretch it depends on how hard the last recall was.

## Why the cards work

The Review view does that stretching. A card graded Good comes back after a longer gap, one graded Again comes back today, and the schedule is written onto the card's own line. The cards from this note are in [[cards/Remembering]]; the paper's are in [[papers/vaswani-2017/cards]].
