# Open Questions

Things [[Sleep and Memory]] does not settle.

- Does the effect survive a normal week, or does it need an unusually protected night?
- Is the nap effect the same mechanism or a different one wearing the same result?
- Every study here measures recall the next morning. What about a month later?
- Do the nap studies measure the same thing the nap claim asserts, or something adjacent?

> [!claim] Self-experiments cannot separate consolidation from practice effects.
> Which is a reason to be careful about what [[Nap Experiment]] can conclude, not a reason to skip it. ^selfexp

The study that follows recall past a week is on the [[Reading Queue]].
